POPULATION & HEALTH ATLAS · 2000–2022

How population and health outcomes
move together, country by country.

A working dashboard for exploring life expectancy, mortality, and health spending across 20 countries. Built as a data-analyst portfolio piece.

Countries shown

Charts update as you check or uncheck countries. Six are selected by default to keep the lines readable.

Life expectancy, 2000–2022

FIG. 01

Years of life expectancy at birth. The dip around 2020–2021 across most countries traces the pandemic's mortality shock — some recovered by 2022, others hadn't yet.

Spending vs. outcome

FIG. 02

Each circle is a country in 2022. Position shows health spending per person against life expectancy; circle size is population. Spending buys the most life expectancy at the low end — the curve flattens hard past roughly $2,000 per capita.

Under-5 mortality

FIG. 03

Deaths per 1,000 live births before age five. Sorted from highest to lowest in the selected year.
